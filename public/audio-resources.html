Strict acceptance rule

These are discovery links, not pre-approved licenses. Review the exact recording's license, attribution, commercial-use permission and right to host/stream it on Minglora. Only verified real-world recordings can be accepted. AI voices, robotic voices, synthesized tones, oscillator sounds and procedural effects are rejected.

No recording is considered installed until its original file is present, its SHA-256 checksum matches, its provenance and rights are recorded, and a person has listened to and tested playback.
